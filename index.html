THE NIGHT

You are stuck in a chair in the den from 12:00 AM to 6:00 AM — about 7 real minutes. Survive to 6 AM to clear the night. Five nights total.

The storm killed the grid. The house runs on a backup battery, and the door, the curtain and the tablet all drain it. At 0% everything opens, the lights die, and something comes down the hall. Power is the real enemy.

CONTROLS

  • Drag to look around. Look far left or right to lean and see more.
  • Door switch — left wall. Shuts the hallway door. D
  • Curtain switch — right wall. Covers the window. C
  • Tablet — on the desk. Tap to raise the security cameras, tap the 5 bezel buttons to change feed, tap the red strip to lower it. Space 1–5 Esc
  • Pause P · this screen H

WHO IS OUT THERE

  • CHOMPY — walks garage → kitchen → living room → hallway → your door. Footsteps mark every move. Shut the door when they get close; he thumps twice and leaves.
  • COB — only moves while her room is not on the raised tablet. Watching her freezes her. A closed door stops her at the doorstep. Night 1+
  • BOO — wanders in on his own schedule and stops the night dead: the clock, the battery and everything hunting you all freeze while he waits. He wants one thing. Pet him. The bar is your time. Night 2+
  • MAYU — you do not meet Mayu. She is what walks in when Boo asks and is refused. There is no counter. Pet the dog.
  • GOLDEN COB — appears inside the room with a burst of static. Do not look at her. Staring kills in about 2 seconds, and she kills anyway if she is still there after about 10. Raise the tablet to make her vanish. Night 3+

SCORE

Nights cleared × 10,000  +  seconds survived × 10  +  battery % left at each 6 AM × 20.

Hoarding power beats hiding behind a closed door. Dying submits your score to the board — RETRY continues the same run, PLAY from the title starts over at Night 1.

Timers are randomised every night, so there is no pattern to memorise. Headphones strongly recommended — most of the warnings are sounds.
